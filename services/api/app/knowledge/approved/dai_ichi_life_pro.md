# Dai-ichi Life Pro

Dai-ichi Life Pro is the flagship endowment plan of Dai-ichi Life Insurance Myanmar. It puts three things in one policy: protection against death and total permanent disability, a living benefit paid in cash once every three years, and a guaranteed maturity benefit at the end of the policy term. It is the base plan to which the Dai-ichi Guard and Dai-ichi CI Plus riders can be attached.

## Eligibility

Entry age is 18 to 59 years old at policy start.

The customer selects a policy term of 6, 9, 12, or 15 years. The premium payment term matches the chosen policy term, so a 12-year policy is paid over 12 years.

The minimum sum insured is MMK 5,000,000. There is no published maximum; larger sums insured are subject to the insurer's underwriting limits and financial assessment.

Dai-ichi Life Pro is a base plan and can be sold on its own. Dai-ichi Guard and Dai-ichi CI Plus are riders and cannot be sold without a Dai-ichi Life Pro base policy.

## Benefits

- Death or total permanent disability benefit of 100% of the sum insured.
- Living benefit of 3% of the sum insured, returned once every three years while the policy stays in force.
- Guaranteed maturity benefit paid at the end of the policy term.
- Premiums paid may be declared as personal income tax relief, subject to Myanmar tax law.
- Total protection rises to 200% of the sum insured when the Dai-ichi Guard rider is added.
- Total protection reaches up to 300% of the sum insured when both Dai-ichi Guard and Dai-ichi CI Plus riders are added.

## Coverage

The death and total permanent disability benefit applies for the whole policy term once the policy is in force and premiums are up to date.

The living benefit falls due at the end of every third policy year. On a 15-year term that means payments at the end of years 3, 6, 9, and 12, with the amount falling due at year 15 settled together with the maturity benefit. The living benefit is paid in cash and does not reduce the death benefit or the maturity benefit.

The guaranteed maturity benefit is paid when the life insured is alive at the end of the policy term and all premiums have been paid.

Illustrative for this demo: a claim for death or total permanent disability should be notified to Dai-ichi Life Insurance Myanmar within 90 days of the event, and total permanent disability must have continued for at least 6 consecutive months before the benefit is assessed.

## Riders and Combinations

Dai-ichi Life Pro can be presented at three levels of cover:

- Base plan only: 100% of the sum insured for death or total permanent disability, plus living and maturity benefits.
- Base plan with Dai-ichi Guard: doubles the death and total permanent disability protection to 200% of the sum insured at a low additional premium.
- Base plan with Dai-ichi Guard and Dai-ichi CI Plus: up to 300% total cover, adding early-stage and late-stage critical illness protection.

Riders share the base policy's owner and life insured. A rider ends when the base policy ends, is surrendered, or lapses.

## Premium and Payment

Illustrative for this demo: premiums may be paid annually, semi-annually, quarterly, or monthly. A grace period of 30 days applies after each due date, and cover continues during the grace period.

If a premium is still unpaid after the grace period the policy lapses. A lapsed policy may be reinstated within 2 years of the first unpaid premium, subject to payment of arrears and satisfactory evidence of insurability.

A free-look period of 15 days from policy delivery lets the customer cancel and receive a refund of premium, less any medical expenses incurred.

## Exclusions

Dai-ichi Life Pro does not pay:

- Death by suicide within 13 months of the policy start date or of any reinstatement date.
- Total permanent disability resulting from self-inflicted injury or attempted suicide.
- Any claim arising from material misstatement or non-disclosure of health, occupation, or lifestyle facts at application.
- Claims arising from war, invasion, or active participation in armed conflict or civil commotion.
- Claims arising while the life insured is committing or attempting to commit a criminal offence.

Dai-ichi Life Pro is not a medical plan. It does not reimburse hospital room charges, surgical fees, outpatient treatment, or any other medical bill.

Agents must state the exclusions when presenting this plan and must never present an exclusion as covered.

## Agent Guidance

A 30-year-old working adult is comfortably within the eligible entry age of 18 to 59 years old, so Dai-ichi Life Pro is the base insurance plan to open the conversation with. It is generally suitable when the customer wants disciplined long-term savings with a guaranteed return alongside life protection, and values receiving cash back during the term rather than only at the end.

Match the policy term to a real goal. A 6-year term suits a short savings target, while 12 or 15 years suits retirement build-up or a long family protection horizon.

Move the conversation elsewhere when the need is different. Accident-only cover at low cost points to Dai-ichi Active Care. A child's education fund points to Htar-Wa-Ra Edu Goal. Hospital and medical bill cover is not offered by this plan.

If the customer asks for a premium figure, an underwriting decision, or a claim outcome, refer to the official illustration and the insurer's processes. Do not quote figures that are not in this document.
