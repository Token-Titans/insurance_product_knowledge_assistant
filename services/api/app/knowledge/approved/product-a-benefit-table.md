# Product A Benefit Table

This table summarises Product A limits for sales-agent product knowledge. Figures are plan limits, not a customer quote.

## Hospital Benefits

| Benefit | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Room and board | RM300 per day, maximum 120 days per policy year |
| Intensive care unit (ICU) | RM600 per day, maximum 30 days per policy year |
| Hospital miscellaneous charges | RM15,000 per admission |
| Surgical fees | Up to RM25,000 per policy year according to the surgical schedule |
| Inpatient specialist visit | RM150 per visit, maximum 10 visits per admission |

Hospitalisation waiting period: 30 days from policy start, except accidents (no waiting period).

## Other Benefits

| Benefit | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Ambulance to hospital for a covered admission | RM250 per admission |
| Outpatient follow-up within 31 days after a covered discharge | RM500 per policy year |

Outpatient follow-up is payable only after a covered inpatient admission. It is not a standalone outpatient plan.

## Conditions And Limits

- Annual aggregate limit for all Product A benefits: RM100,000 per insured person per policy year.
- Room and board is not payable for day-care or outpatient procedures.
- ICU days count toward the ICU day limit, not the standard room-and-board day limit.
- All amounts are before any deductible. Product A has no deductible on the base hospital plan.

## Exclusions Summary

Not covered: undisclosed pre-existing conditions, cosmetic procedures, pregnancy and childbirth, mental or behavioural disorders (base plan), treatment outside Malaysia except short-trip emergency care, and experimental treatment. See the Product A Brochure exclusions section for the approved wording.
