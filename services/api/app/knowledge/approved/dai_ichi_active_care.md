# Dai-ichi Active Care

Dai-ichi Active Care is the personal accident plan of Dai-ichi Life Insurance Myanmar. It pays out when an accident causes death, total permanent disability, or dismemberment, and it adds daily cash while the insured is hospitalised because of an accident. It is the lowest-cost way for an agent to give a customer meaningful protection, and unlike Dai-ichi Guard and Dai-ichi CI Plus it is a standalone plan that needs no base policy.

## Eligibility

Age of the insured is 18 to 64 years old at policy start, which is a wider age range than Dai-ichi Life Pro allows.

Policy term is 1 to 15 years and the premium payment term matches the policy term. A 1-year term makes this the only plan in the range that can be sold as short-term cover.

The minimum sum insured is MMK 5,000,000.

Dai-ichi Active Care is a standalone plan. It may be sold on its own or held alongside a Dai-ichi Life Pro policy.

## Benefits

- 100% of the sum insured on accidental death or accidental total permanent disability.
- Additional 100% of the sum insured for accidental death while travelling as a passenger on public conveyance, giving 200% in total for that event.
- Accidental dismemberment benefit, paid as a percentage of the sum insured according to the scale of dismemberment in the policy.
- 0.2% of the sum insured per day for hospitalisation due to an accident.
- Additional 0.2% of the sum insured per day for intensive care unit treatment due to an accident, giving 0.4% per day in total.
- Return of premium on non-accidental death or non-accidental total permanent disability, available on policy terms of 5 years and above.

## Coverage

Cover applies to accidental causes. An accident means a sudden, unforeseen, and involuntary external event, and the death, disability, or injury must result from it.

The public conveyance benefit applies when the insured is travelling as a fare-paying passenger on a licensed bus, train, ferry, or commercial aircraft. It does not apply to the insured while driving or riding their own vehicle.

Daily hospitalisation cash is a fixed percentage of the sum insured and is not a reimbursement of hospital charges. Illustrative for this demo: on a sum insured of MMK 10,000,000 the accident hospitalisation benefit is MMK 20,000 per day, rising to MMK 40,000 per day while in intensive care, payable for up to 60 days per accident.

The return of premium feature means that if the insured dies or becomes totally permanently disabled from a cause that is not an accident, and the policy term is 5 years or more, the premiums paid are returned instead of the accident sum insured.

Illustrative for this demo: there is no waiting period for accident benefits, cover starts from the policy start date, and a claim should be notified within 30 days of the accident.

## Premium and Payment

Illustrative for this demo: premiums may be paid annually, semi-annually, quarterly, or monthly, with a 30-day grace period after each due date. Premium depends on the sum insured, the policy term, and the occupational class of the insured.

Occupational class matters on this plan. Manual, industrial, and driving occupations attract a higher rate than office-based work, and a change of occupation must be notified to the insurer.

## Exclusions

Dai-ichi Active Care does not pay for:

- Illness, disease, or any naturally occurring medical condition, since this is an accident plan and not a medical or life plan.
- Suicide, attempted suicide, or intentional self-inflicted injury.
- Accidents while under the influence of alcohol or of drugs not prescribed by a registered doctor.
- Accidents arising from professional sport, motor racing, mountaineering, scuba diving, and similar hazardous activities.
- Accidents while flying other than as a passenger on a licensed commercial flight.
- War, invasion, active participation in armed conflict, civil commotion, or riot.
- Accidents arising while the insured is committing or attempting to commit a criminal offence.
- Pregnancy, childbirth, and related complications.

Dai-ichi Active Care does not reimburse medical bills. Surgical fees, medicine, and hospital charges are not paid; only the fixed daily accident hospitalisation cash applies.

Agents must be clear that a customer who falls ill rather than suffering an accident is not covered by this plan.

## Agent Guidance

A 30-year-old customer is well within the 18 to 64 age range, and Dai-ichi Active Care is often the most suitable first step for someone whose budget cannot yet carry an endowment premium. It is also the natural fit for a customer who rides a motorcycle daily, travels frequently for work, or works in a physical trade.

Use the 1-year term when the customer wants to try the product or needs cover for a fixed project or trip. Use 5 years or more when the return of premium feature matters to them.

State the boundary of the plan early. This is accident cover only, so a customer worried about cancer belongs in a Dai-ichi CI Plus conversation and a customer wanting savings belongs in a Dai-ichi Life Pro conversation.

Ask about occupation before discussing price, because occupational class changes the premium.
