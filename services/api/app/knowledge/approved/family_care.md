# Family Care

Family Care is a medical plan for employed adults and their eligible dependents. This brochure is an approved product-knowledge source for sales agents. It is not a policy contract, a quote, or an underwriting decision.

## Eligibility

Family Care is available to working adults aged 18 to 60 at policy start. Cover may be renewed up to age 65, subject to the insurer’s renewal terms.

A 30-year-old employed adult is within the eligible entry age. Dependents (spouse aged 18 to 60 and children aged 15 days to 18 years) may be included on the same plan.

This plan is generally suitable when the customer wants hospital bill reimbursement plus limited outpatient GP cover for a family.

## Benefits

- Room and board up to RM300 per day for a standard private room, maximum 120 days per policy year.
- Intensive care unit (ICU) charges up to RM600 per day, maximum 30 days per policy year.
- Hospital miscellaneous charges up to RM15,000 per admission.
- Surgical fees according to the Family Care surgical schedule, up to RM25,000 per policy year.
- Outpatient GP visits up to RM50 per visit, maximum 10 visits per policy year.
- Outpatient follow-up within 31 days after a covered discharge, up to RM500 per policy year.

## Coverage

Family Care pays medically necessary inpatient treatment of a covered illness or injury, plus the outpatient GP benefit above. Hospitalisation benefits begin after a waiting period of 30 days from the policy start date, except for accidents, which have no waiting period.

Pre-authorisation is required for planned (non-emergency) admissions at least 48 hours before hospitalisation. Emergency admissions must be notified within 24 hours.

The annual aggregate limit is RM100,000 per insured person per policy year.

## Exclusions

Family Care does not pay for:

- Pre-existing conditions that were not disclosed and accepted at application.
- Cosmetic or aesthetic procedures.
- Pregnancy, childbirth, miscarriage, and related complications.
- Mental or behavioural disorders on the base plan.
- Treatment outside Malaysia, except emergency treatment during travel of up to 30 consecutive days.
- Experimental or unproven treatments.

Agents must not present an exclusion as cover.
