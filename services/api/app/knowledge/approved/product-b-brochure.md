# Product B Brochure

Product B is a group hospital cash plan. It pays a fixed daily amount during covered inpatient confinement. It does not reimburse hospital bills. This brochure is an approved product-knowledge source for sales agents.

## Hospital Benefits

Product B pays a hospital cash benefit of RM150 per day when the insured person is confined as an inpatient for a covered illness or injury.

- Daily hospital cash: RM150 per day, maximum 90 days per policy year.
- ICU cash: an additional RM150 per day (RM300 total for that day) for ICU confinement, maximum 15 ICU days per policy year.
- Surgical cash: a once-per-admission lump sum of RM1,000 when a covered inpatient surgery is performed.

Hospital cash begins from the first day of inpatient confinement after a waiting period of 60 days from the policy start date. Accidents have a waiting period of 24 hours.

Product B does not pay room-and-board reimbursement, hospital miscellaneous charges, or surgical fee schedules. Those bill-reimbursement benefits belong to Product A, not Product B.

## Eligibility

Product B is available to employed adults aged 18 to 55 at policy start. Cover may be renewed up to age 60, subject to the insurer’s renewal terms.

## Important Conditions

Payment requires a hospitalisation of at least 24 consecutive hours. Day-care surgery does not qualify.

Only one daily cash amount is paid per calendar day, even if the person is transferred between wards.

Hospital cash is paid to the insured person. It is not an indemnity of hospital invoices.

## Exclusions

Product B does not pay hospital cash for:

- Pre-existing conditions that were not disclosed and accepted at application.
- Confinement for routine check-ups, rest cures, or convalescence without active treatment.
- Pregnancy, childbirth, and related confinement.
- Cosmetic procedures.
- Hospitalisation outside Malaysia.
