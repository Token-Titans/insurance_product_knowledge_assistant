# Income Protect

Income Protect pays a monthly income benefit when the insured person is totally unable to work due to a covered illness or injury. This brochure is an approved product-knowledge source for sales agents. It is not an investment product and does not replace medical cover.

## Eligibility

Income Protect is available to employed adults aged 21 to 55 at policy start who are in full-time paid work. Cover may be renewed up to age 60.

A 30-year-old employed adult is within the eligible entry age. This plan is generally suitable when the customer’s main concern is replacing salary during a long disability, not paying hospital bills. Medical hospitalisation cover remains a separate product such as Family Care.

## Benefits

- Monthly disability income of RM3,000, payable after a 90-day waiting (elimination) period.
- Benefit payment period up to 24 months per claim, while the insured remains totally disabled.
- Waiver of premium while a valid monthly benefit is being paid.

## Coverage

Total disability means the insured person cannot perform the material duties of their own occupation for the first 12 months of a claim, and any occupation thereafter, as defined in the policy wording.

The monthly benefit stops at recovery, at the end of the 24-month payment period, at age 60, or at death, whichever is earlier.

This product does not pay hospital room and board, outpatient GP visits, or hospital cash.

## Exclusions

Income Protect does not pay for disability caused by:

- Pre-existing conditions that were not disclosed and accepted at application.
- Self-inflicted injury.
- Participation in a criminal act.
- War or civil commotion.
- Unemployment that is not caused by a covered disability.
