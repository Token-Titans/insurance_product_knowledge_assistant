# Product A Brochure

Product A is a group hospital care plan for employed adults. This brochure is an approved product-knowledge source for sales agents. It is not a policy contract and does not replace underwriting or a customer-specific quote.

## Hospital Benefits

Product A pays hospitalisation benefits when the insured person is admitted as an inpatient for medically necessary treatment of a covered illness or injury.

Covered hospitalisation benefits include:

- Room and board up to RM300 per day for a standard private room, for a maximum of 120 days per policy year.
- Intensive care unit (ICU) charges up to RM600 per day, for a maximum of 30 days per policy year.
- Hospital miscellaneous charges (including in-hospital diagnostic tests, prescribed inpatient medicines, and operating theatre fees) up to RM15,000 per admission.
- Surgical fees according to the Product A surgical schedule, up to RM25,000 per policy year.

Hospitalisation benefits begin only after a waiting period of 30 days from the policy start date, except for accidents, which have no waiting period.

Daily room and board is paid only for days of actual inpatient confinement. Day-care surgery and outpatient treatment are not hospitalisation benefits under Product A.

## Eligibility

Product A is available to employed adults aged 18 to 60 at policy start. Cover may be renewed up to age 65, subject to the insurer’s renewal terms.

Dependents are not included in the base Product A plan. A separate rider, if offered, would be required and is not described in this brochure.

## Important Conditions

Hospitalisation benefits are payable only for medically necessary inpatient treatment of a covered cause. Admission must be recommended by a registered medical practitioner.

Pre-authorisation is required for planned (non-emergency) admissions at least 48 hours before hospitalisation. Emergency admissions must be notified within 24 hours of admission.

Benefits are subject to the annual limits in the Product A benefit table. Amounts above those limits are not payable.

## Exclusions

Product A does not pay hospitalisation benefits for:

- Pre-existing conditions that were not disclosed and accepted at application.
- Cosmetic or aesthetic procedures, including related hospital stays.
- Pregnancy, childbirth, miscarriage, and related complications.
- Mental or behavioural disorders, except where specifically listed in an approved rider (none is included in the base plan).
- Treatment outside Malaysia, except for emergency treatment during travel of up to 30 consecutive days.
- Hospitalisation arising from war, civil commotion, or participation in a criminal act.
- Experimental or unproven treatments.

This list is not a complete policy wording. Agents must not present an exclusion as cover, and must not invent additional exclusions.
