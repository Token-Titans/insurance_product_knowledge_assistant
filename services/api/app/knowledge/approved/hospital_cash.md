# Hospital Cash

Hospital Cash pays a fixed amount for each day of covered inpatient confinement. It is not an indemnity of hospital invoices. This brochure is an approved product-knowledge source for sales agents.

## Eligibility

Hospital Cash is available to employed adults aged 18 to 55 at policy start. Cover may be renewed up to age 60.

A 30-year-old employed adult is within the eligible entry age. This plan is generally suitable when the customer wants extra cash during a hospital stay rather than bill reimbursement. It can sit beside Family Care; it does not replace Family Care hospital benefits.

## Benefits

- Daily hospital cash of RM150 per day, maximum 90 days per policy year.
- Additional ICU cash of RM150 per ICU day (RM300 total for that day), maximum 15 ICU days per policy year.
- Surgical cash of RM1,000 once per covered inpatient surgery admission.

## Coverage

Payment requires hospitalisation of at least 24 consecutive hours. Day-care surgery does not qualify.

Waiting period: 60 days from policy start for illness; 24 hours for accidents.

Only one daily cash amount is paid per calendar day. The annual aggregate cash limit is RM20,000 per insured person per policy year.

## Exclusions

Hospital Cash does not pay for:

- Pre-existing conditions that were not disclosed and accepted at application.
- Confinement for routine check-ups, rest cures, or convalescence without active treatment.
- Pregnancy, childbirth, and related confinement.
- Cosmetic procedures.
- Hospitalisation outside Malaysia.

Hospital Cash does not reimburse room charges, hospital miscellaneous bills, or surgical fee schedules.
