# Product B Benefit Table

This table summarises Product B hospital-cash limits for sales-agent product knowledge.

## Hospital Benefits

| Benefit | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Daily hospital cash | RM150 per day, maximum 90 days per policy year |
| ICU cash (additional) | RM150 extra per ICU day, maximum 15 ICU days per policy year |
| Surgical cash | RM1,000 per covered inpatient surgery admission |

Waiting period: 60 days for illness, 24 hours for accident.

Minimum confinement: 24 consecutive hours.

## Conditions And Limits

- Annual aggregate cash limit: RM20,000 per insured person per policy year.
- Product B does not reimburse hospital bills, room charges, or surgical fee schedules.
- Day-care and outpatient treatment are not covered.

## Exclusions Summary

Not covered: undisclosed pre-existing conditions, pregnancy and childbirth, cosmetic procedures, rest cures, and hospitalisation outside Malaysia. See the Product B Brochure exclusions section.
