# Dai-ichi CI Plus

Dai-ichi CI Plus is the critical illness rider from Dai-ichi Life Insurance Myanmar. It pays a lump sum when the life insured is diagnosed with a covered critical illness such as cancer, kidney disease, or lung disease, so that the family can meet treatment costs and lost income without breaking their savings. The money is paid on diagnosis and is not tied to hospital invoices.

## Eligibility

Entry age is 18 to 59 years old at rider start.

Policy term is 5 to 15 years and the premium payment term matches the policy term.

The minimum sum insured is MMK 5,000,000.

Dai-ichi CI Plus is available only as a rider purchased alongside a Dai-ichi Life Pro base policy. It cannot be sold as a standalone policy.

## Benefits

- Early-stage critical illness benefit of 25% of the sum insured.
- Late-stage critical illness benefit of 100% of the sum insured, less any benefit already paid under this rider.
- Death or total permanent disability benefit of 100% of the sum insured, less any benefit already paid under this rider.
- Paid as a lump sum on diagnosis, so the customer may use it for treatment, travel, or replacing lost income.
- Rider premiums may be declared as personal income tax relief, subject to Myanmar tax law.

## Coverage

Benefits under this rider are cumulative but capped. The 25% early-stage payment is deducted from any later late-stage or death payment, so the rider pays a maximum of 100% of the sum insured in total. Once 100% has been paid the rider ends.

Worked example, illustrative for this demo: on a sum insured of MMK 20,000,000, an early-stage cancer diagnosis pays MMK 5,000,000. If the condition later reaches late stage, the rider pays MMK 15,000,000, bringing the total to MMK 20,000,000 and closing the rider.

Illustrative for this demo: covered conditions are grouped into early-stage and late-stage definitions and include cancer, kidney disease, lung disease, heart disease, and stroke. A waiting period of 90 days from the rider start date applies to all critical illness benefits, and the life insured must survive 14 days after diagnosis for the benefit to become payable. Diagnosis must be confirmed in writing by a registered specialist.

The rider is tied to the base policy and ends when the base Dai-ichi Life Pro policy matures, is surrendered, or lapses.

## Premium and Payment

Illustrative for this demo: the rider premium is collected together with the base Dai-ichi Life Pro premium on the same due date and payment frequency, and is not billed separately. The grace period and reinstatement rules of the base policy apply.

## Exclusions

Dai-ichi CI Plus does not pay:

- Any critical illness diagnosed during the 90-day waiting period from the rider start date.
- Pre-existing conditions and any signs or symptoms present before the rider start date that were not disclosed and accepted at application.
- Any condition that does not meet the rider's written definition for its stage, even where a doctor uses the same everyday name for the illness.
- Illness arising from alcohol or drug abuse, or from a self-inflicted act.
- Illness related to human immunodeficiency virus infection, except where the rider's occupational infection provision applies.
- Congenital conditions.

Dai-ichi CI Plus is not medical insurance. It does not reimburse hospital bills, room charges, surgical fees, medicine, or outpatient treatment. It pays a fixed percentage of the sum insured regardless of the actual cost of treatment.

Agents must present the waiting period, the survival period, and the deduction of earlier payments whenever they present this rider.

## Agent Guidance

Use Dai-ichi CI Plus when the customer's stated fear is the cost of a serious illness rather than the cost of dying. It suits a customer who is the household earner, has a family history of cancer or heart disease, or has savings that a long illness would quickly consume.

Explain the two-stage design plainly. Early detection releases 25% of the sum insured for treatment while the customer is still working, and the balance remains available if the condition worsens.

Correct the common misunderstanding directly: this rider does not pay hospital bills. It pays a fixed lump sum on diagnosis, which the customer can spend as they choose.

Where the customer wants both illness money and higher death cover, present Dai-ichi CI Plus together with Dai-ichi Guard on the Dai-ichi Life Pro base plan for up to 300% total cover.
