# Product FAQ

Approved frequently asked questions for Product A and Product B. Use this with the product brochure and benefit table. Do not treat this FAQ as a policy contract.

## What Is The Difference Between Product A And Product B?

Product A reimburses specified hospital bills (room and board, ICU, hospital miscellaneous charges, and scheduled surgical fees) up to plan limits.

Product B pays a fixed hospital cash amount per day of covered inpatient confinement. It does not pay hospital invoices.

A customer who needs help with hospital bills is generally looking at Product A. A customer who wants extra cash during a hospital stay is generally looking at Product B. InsureAssist does not recommend a purchase or calculate premiums.

## Does Product A Cover Outpatient GP Visits?

No. Product A is a hospital plan. Outpatient GP visits are not covered. A limited outpatient follow-up benefit applies only within 31 days after a covered inpatient discharge, up to RM500 per policy year. See the Product A benefit table.

## How Long Is The Hospitalisation Waiting Period?

Product A: 30 days from policy start for illness; no waiting period for accidents.

Product B: 60 days from policy start for illness; 24 hours for accidents.

Waiting periods apply to hospitalisation benefits described in each product’s brochure.

## Are Pre-Existing Conditions Covered?

Neither Product A nor Product B covers pre-existing conditions that were not disclosed and accepted at application. Agents must not state that a pre-existing condition is covered unless an approved underwriting decision says so. InsureAssist does not make that decision.

## Can InsureAssist Issue A Policy Or Quote A Premium?

No. InsureAssist is a product-knowledge assistant for sales agents. It does not issue policies, calculate premiums, underwrite, or handle claims.
