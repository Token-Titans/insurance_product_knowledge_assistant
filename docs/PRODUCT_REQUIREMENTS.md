# Product Requirements

All requirements below describe the future MVP unless marked otherwise. The current repository is foundation-only.

## Must have

- Ask a natural-language question about a supported insurance product.
- Retrieve relevant knowledge from approved product documents.
- Generate a clear, understandable, grounded answer.
- Identify relevant important conditions and exclusions.
- Show the relevant source/reference.
- Explicitly acknowledge unavailable information.

## Should have

- Compare supported products.
- Suggest useful sales questions.
- Produce a simple customer-friendly explanation.
- Trigger follow-up automation.

## Nice to have

- Voice.
- Multilingual answers.
- Conversation history.
- Recommended talking points.

## Out of scope

- Customer-facing chatbot.
- Claims assistance.
- Underwriting assistance.
- Quotation or premium calculation.
- Policy issuance.
- Payment.
- CRM.
- Lead management.
- Authentication.
- Full document management.
- Enterprise search.
- Autonomous sales agent.

No additional product requirement is implied by architecture diagrams, API examples, UI scaffolding, or implementation details.
