# Demo questions

Select a product in the picker first, then tap a chip or paste a question.

Product ids:

| Product | `product_id` |
| --- | --- |
| Dai-ichi Life Pro | `dai-ichi-life-pro` |
| Dai-ichi Guard | `dai-ichi-guard` |
| Dai-ichi CI Plus | `dai-ichi-ci-plus` |
| Dai-ichi Active Care | `dai-ichi-active-care` |
| Htar-Wa-Ra Edu Goal | `htar-wa-ra-edu-goal` |

## On-screen chips (any product)

1. What is the entry age and minimum sum insured?
2. What exclusions apply to this plan?
3. What is the cryptocurrency wallet protection sublimit?

The third question should come back as unknown. The approved documents do not cover cryptocurrency wallets.

## Dai-ichi Life Pro — `dai-ichi-life-pro`

- What living benefit does Dai-ichi Life Pro pay, and how often?
- Can Dai-ichi Guard or Dai-ichi CI Plus be sold without Life Pro?
- What happens if I add both Guard and CI Plus — how much total cover?
- Is there a suicide exclusion, and for how long?

## Dai-ichi Guard — `dai-ichi-guard`

- Is Dai-ichi Guard a standalone plan or a rider?
- Does Guard pay a living benefit or maturity value?
- How does Guard double the death cover with Life Pro?

## Dai-ichi CI Plus — `dai-ichi-ci-plus`

- What does early-stage critical illness pay versus late-stage?
- Does CI Plus reimburse hospital bills?
- What is the waiting period for critical illness benefits?

## Dai-ichi Active Care — `dai-ichi-active-care`

- Does Active Care cover illness, or only accidents?
- What extra benefit applies if death happens on public transport?
- How much daily hospital cash is paid after an accident?

## Htar-Wa-Ra Edu Goal — `htar-wa-ra-edu-goal`

- What is the difference between the Double Benefit Plan and the Basic Benefit Plan?
- What is the waiver of premium benefit?
- Who is the life insured — the parent or the child?

## Suggested demo order

1. Life Pro — living benefit
2. Any product — exclusions chip
3. Any product — cryptocurrency sublimit (unknown)
4. Active Care — illness or accident?
5. Edu Goal — waiver of premium

Keep answers grounded in the approved sample documents. They are not official policy wording.
